# Register now for the 2026 Symposium!

Registration is free for all registrants! MDLS 2026 will be an in-person event September 28-30 at Purdue University.   
Please note, spots are limited and may fill up quickly. There will be a waitlist available.  
- Fill out the [registration form](https://forms.gle/TGR7vTwp76DciG9u9) to register for MDLS 2026.  

## About MDLS 2026
- MDLS 2026 will be an in-person event September 28-30.  
- All sessions will take place at Purdue University, with 60 seats available. A waiting list will be maintained should seat requests fill up.  
- The final schedule will be posted to the MDLS website in late August.  

## About MDLS

The Midwest Data Librarian Symposium is intended to provide librarians who support research data management and research data services the chance to network and expand their research data-related knowledge base and skill sets. MDLS 2026 is open to all who wish to attend, including those from the Midwest and beyond, as well as librarians in training.

We hope to see you in September!
